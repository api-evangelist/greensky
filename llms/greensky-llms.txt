# GreenSky

> GreenSky is a financial-technology company that powers point-of-sale consumer financing. Through a network of bank partners and enrolled merchants it lets consumers apply for and use installment loans and revolving credit at the point of sale, concentrated in home-improvement and elective-healthcare financing. GreenSky Servicing, LLC (NMLS #1416362) services the loan program. GreenSky is a consumer/merchant fintech with no public developer API surface; this profile is maintained by the API Evangelist network.
